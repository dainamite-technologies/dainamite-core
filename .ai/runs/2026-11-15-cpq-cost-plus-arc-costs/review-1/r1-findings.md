## Summary

**Counts:** 0 blocker, 2 major (#1, #4), 5 minor (#2, #3, #6, #7, #8), 1 nit (#5).

**Checked and found clean:**
- **Lock order.** Activation takes the order row, then `lockSourceQuote`, then `lockSubscriptions` (sorted, `refresh: true`) on the new and ARC paths, before any cost read or write. Re-confirmation takes the quote lock first and reads the order row unlocked. Refresh and conversion take only the quote lock. No other PESSIMISTIC lock exists on these rows, so I found no inversion or deadlock path.
- **COST_SOURCE_CHANGED placement.** At conversion it runs after the R7 blockers and before request cancellation and order creation. At activation it runs after the subscription locks and before `applyLineChanges` and the cost writes. The hash projection normalises decimals and dates and excludes bookkeeping. Set and stale-key computation are consistent between carry and guard. Markers are server-owned (`stripArcMarkers` on client input) and sticky.
- **Carry and write-back.** The `carryItemCosts` field list matches the spec, including FX snapshot, index_snapshot, supplier, validity, term and min term. QUOTE_ONLY_ARC_KEYS gains both markers, so the I-9 payload is unchanged. `inventory_cost_id` is linked at conversion and asserted against the unique (item, key) row. Ended keys are reactivated in place (Renew and Amend apply items in place). Full-image replace applies only to marked lines. Carried rows survive frozen-line recalculations: the drop applies only to non-carried rows on unreconfigured mirrors, and R1a drops catalog rows only.
- **Renew.** Match / carried / drop / instantiate, the due rule (MRC, carried value, no answer, `valid_until` / `supplier_term_end` < `newTermEnd`) and un-flagging all check out. Price-basis due blocks conversion through R7. Keep current enforces allowed fields, evidence and dates ≥ new term end, is lock-exempt and leaves values untouched. Standalone-only un-freeze is correct, and Phase 1 frozen and unmarked lines keep the old rules.
- **Re-confirmation.** Feature-gated (`cpq.orders.manage` + `cpq.costs.manage`, toggle) with mutation guards. It refuses cancelled and active / fulfilled orders, never touches charges or totals, recomputes `total_amount` with `costLineTotal`, emits no event, uses an amount-free command log and redacts responses.
- **Cancel exposure.** Reference date rules, end-of-month month counting, unknown term for MRC only, no amounts.
- **Migration20261115151709.** Up and down widen and restore both CHECKs. Every written origin / value_origin value is allowed (order and inventory tables carry no origin CHECK). The partial index matches the usage semi-join. Usage SQL is scoped and parameterized via the QB.
- **Confidentiality of the costs reads.** `previous`, `droppedItemCosts`, `exposure` and `arcCosts` are returned only to holders. Flags-only for everyone else, apart from #8.
- **UI.** Uses apiCall, Button, lucide icons and t(). Dialogs handle Ctrl/Cmd+Enter and Escape (Radix). ArcLineDiff currencies are consistent.
- **Seed and E2E.** The seed goes through the real services and is idempotent by scenario. The E2E matches the real routes (`from-subscription` `quoteId`, pending → active).
